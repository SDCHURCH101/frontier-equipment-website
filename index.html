Heritage · Alaska since the 1970s

Builder of the North Slope.

Frontier ran with the first generation of the North Slope. Under the Frontier Companies of Alaska, the name built the biggest share of the region's roads, including the Spine Road this property sits on, and raised the docks, airstrips, camps, and shops that opened Prudhoe Bay. The orange and black F has been on Alaska trucks and buildings for half a century.

In 1982 Frontier opened a new $4,000,000 heavy equipment maintenance facility at Prudhoe Bay, built to service, repair, and overhaul up to 23 pieces of heavy construction equipment at one time. That same proven North Slope infrastructure is what Frontier Equipment Company leases today.

The Future of Alaska

Positioned for what comes next.

The North Slope is entering a sustained build-out cycle. Several demand drivers are competing for the same finite pool of permitted pad and shop space, and there is no equivalent asset coming to market.

01

Oil & gas development

Continued investment across the central North Slope and NPRA drives demand for staging, maintenance, and field support pads.

02

TAPS pipeline activity

Trans-Alaska Pipeline maintenance, integrity work, and tie-in projects need nearby shop and yard capacity year round.

03

Arctic data centers

The Slope's natural cooling and stranded-power potential is drawing data center developers who need industrial pad space.

04

Cold-climate compute

Arctic compute and hosting deployments need permitted pads and shop infrastructure to scale in a cold-climate advantage.

05

Support & service industry

Trucking, mechanical, fabrication, environmental, and logistics firms all require Slope-based shop and yard footprints.

06

Defense & sovereign infrastructure

A sharper Arctic strategic focus is expanding demand for resilient, road-connected industrial real estate.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Where is Frontier Pad located?

Frontier Pad is 12.5 miles west of Deadhorse, Alaska, on the Spine Road, inside Hilcorp security, on the North Slope near Prudhoe Bay.

What does Frontier Equipment Company lease?

We lease shop space, gravel pad and yard space, and man camp (workforce housing) beds at Prudhoe Bay. Tenants can take a single shop, a block of pad, beds in the camp, or the integrated footprint together.

How large is the shop space?

How many beds are in the camp?

Yes. Frontier Pad is inside Hilcorp's security perimeter, road-connected on the Spine Road, gated, and operable in every season including winter campaigns.
